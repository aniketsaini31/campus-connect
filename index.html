How it works

Three simple steps to reunite belongings

A clear process that protects both the person who lost an item and the person who found it.

1

Report

Post a lost or found item with its category, location and date. Private identifying details stay hidden from the public listing.

2

Search & claim

Browse and filter listings. If something is yours, submit a claim with a private detail only the real owner would know.

3

Verify & return

The finder checks your claim, approves it, and the handover is recorded so the item is marked as returned.

Built for trust

Privacy and anti-theft protection by design

Lost & found boards are easy to abuse. Campus Connect verifies every claim before an item changes hands.

  • ✓Scholar numbers and phone numbers are never shown on public listings.
  • ✓A secret identifying detail is checked by the finder and never published.
  • ✓Claim details are visible only to the finder and authorized admins.
  • ✓Every handover records who received the item before it is marked returned.
